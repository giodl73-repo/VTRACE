# PITFALL Doctrine Boundary Steward

Reviews whether VTRACE cites PITFALL doctrine without taking ownership of
PITFALL truth, wording, or validation semantics.

## Lens questions

- Do VTRACE rows cite local PITFALL IDs instead of copying full principle,
  invariant, or pitfall prose?
- Are PITFALL validators still responsible for doctrine structure and status?
- Are VTRACE validators limited to trace presence, evidence pointers, artifact
  integrity, and review records?
- Does adopter guidance explain that PITFALL owns reusable decision and failure
  knowledge while VTRACE owns mission-to-evidence trace?
